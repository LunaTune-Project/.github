LunaTune is an independent software project exploring native, lightweight music applications for Linux, with Android planned.

## Our principles

- **Privacy first:** No LunaTune telemetry, tracking or unnecessary personal data collection.
- **User freedom:** Respect your desktop, existing configurations and control over your system.
- **Backward compatibility:** Preserve user data and support older systems wherever reasonably possible.
- **Open development:** Human-reviewed AI-assisted development, feedback and contributions.
- **Free, now and forever:** No LunaTune paid tiers or donations. Provider subscriptions are separate.

## Community

Visit our [community repository](https://github.com/LunaTune-Project/LunaTune-Community) for community information and policies.

LunaTune is currently in development. Music provider integrations and release readiness remain subject to verification. No official application releases are available yet.
